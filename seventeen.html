My Top 5 SEVENTEEN Songs

October 08, 2026 · 5 min read · by Emmarie

Every CARAT has their own list. And every CARAT will defend it with their whole heart. Mine is full of soft, emotional songs — the ones that feel like a warm hug, a late-night thought, or a quiet cry in the car. These five aren't just my favorites. They're the ones that shaped me.

1. "Circle"

This song is a spiritual experience. The lyrics, the vocals, the build — everything about "Circle" feels like a gentle reminder that we're all connected, that things come back around, that we're never truly alone. It makes me cry in the best way. It's my comfort song.

2. "Pinwheel"

There's something hauntingly beautiful about "Pinwheel." It's quiet, aching, and full of longing — the kind of song that sits with you long after it ends. The way it captures distance and waiting and love… it's a masterpiece. Nobody talks about it enough.

3. "God of Music"

My serotonin song. When I need to feel alive again, this is what I play. It's joy in musical form — bright, hopeful, and so full of love for what they do. It's the song that reminds me why I fell in love with SEVENTEEN in the first place.

4. "Campfire"

"Campfire" feels like sitting outside with the people you love, warm drinks in hand, watching the stars. It's simple and gentle, and it captures that feeling of peace you only get when you're completely safe with someone. It's a hug in song form.

5. "Kidult"

This one hurts in the most beautiful way. "Kidult" is about growing up without losing the child inside you — about being tired but still hopeful. Every time I hear it, I feel seen. It's the song I wish I could thank them for in person.

"Even when I'm tired, I won't give up — because the kid inside me still believes."

So that's my list. Circle, Pinwheel, God of Music, Campfire, and Kidult. If you know, you know. 💎

BIAS

Why Minghao Is My Forever Bias

October 08, 2026 · 5 min read · by Emmarie

There's something about Minghao that feels like a deep breath. In a world that's always rushing, he moves at his own pace — and somehow that's the most magnetic thing about him.

The Dancer

Minghao dances like water. Not in a flashy or loud way, but in a way that feels inevitable — like the movement was always there, waiting to be shown. His contemporary solos hit differently because you can tell he's not performing for anyone. He's just feeling.

The Artist

His paintings, his photography, his eye for aesthetics — it all comes from the same place. He doesn't just see the world; he notices it. The small things. The quiet corners. The things most people walk past.

"I'm not shy. I just think a lot before I speak."

The Savage

And then there's the side that made me absolutely fall in love: Minghao does not hold back. He'll roast a member with zero hesitation, deliver the driest comeback you've ever heard, and then go back to sipping his tea like nothing happened. He has this gift for saying exactly what everyone's thinking — but with the most deadpan face imaginable.

His savage moments aren't mean — they're honest. He just refuses to perform politeness he doesn't feel. And there's something incredibly freeing about watching someone be that unapologetically himself.

The Person

What I love most about Minghao is that he doesn't try to be anything other than himself. He's soft without being weak. Quiet without being shy. Deep without being pretentious. Savage without being cruel. He's taught me that stillness is its own kind of strength — and that sharp honesty is its own kind of love. 💙🌙

UNIT

Best Performance Unit B-Sides

October 08, 2026 · 4 min read · by Emmarie

The Performance Unit — Hoshi, Jun, Minghao, and Dino — is where SEVENTEEN's artistry truly shines. Their B-sides are often quieter, moodier, and more experimental than the title tracks, and that's exactly why I love them.

My Top Picks

1. "Spider" (Hoshi solo) — dark, cinematic, and impossibly cool.

2. "Wave" (Jun solo) — dreamy and gentle, like floating.

3. "Side By Side" (The8 solo) — Minghao's softest, most personal track.

4. "Wait" (Dino solo) — funky and confident, Dino at his best.

5. "Highlight" (Performance Team) — the original, the classic, the one that made me love this unit.

These songs aren't just performances — they're little films. 🩰

MINGHAO

Minghao's Art & Aesthetic

October 08, 2026 · 3 min read · by Emmarie

Minghao doesn't just create — he observes. His paintings, his photography, and even his fashion choices all share the same quiet, thoughtful energy. Nothing is loud. Nothing is rushed. Everything is intentional.

What I Love Most

His paintings often use soft, muted tones — blues, creams, and dusty pinks. They feel like memories rather than images, like something you almost remember from a dream.

His photography is the same. He captures small moments — a shadow on a wall, a cup of tea, a quiet street — and somehow makes them feel important. That's a rare gift.

"Art is how I talk to myself." — Minghao

Whenever I feel overwhelmed, I look at his art and I feel calmer. That's the effect his work has. It slows you down. It makes you breathe. 🎨🌙
