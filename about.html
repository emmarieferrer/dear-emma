About Me

Get to know the girl behind Dear Emma

Hi, I'm Emmarie! 🌸

Welcome to my little corner of the internet. I'm a proud CARAT, someone who loves styling outfits, and someone who enjoys capturing the small, beautiful moments of everyday life.

I started Dear Emma as a way to document my thoughts and share the things I love — from SEVENTEEN comebacks to outfits and everyday living.

When I'm not blogging, you'll find me listening to music, journaling, cleaning, studying, or hunting for the perfect thrift find.

  • 📖 Journaling, reading & watching
  • 👗 Fashion & thrifting
